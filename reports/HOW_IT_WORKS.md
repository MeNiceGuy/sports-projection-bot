# How It Works

The sports projection bot currently builds daily reports by combining public data with simple early-stage factors.

## NBA factors
- team record differential
- home-court bonus
- recent form
- offensive production
- scoring efficiency

## MLB factors
- team record differential
- home-field bonus
- probable pitcher presence
- recent form
- OPS
- ERA
- WHIP

## Current reality
This is a growing projection tool stack, not a finished betting edge engine.
