# Market and Player Props Plan

The bot can now compare model outputs against supplied market lines through `logs/market_lines.csv`.

A player props scaffold has been added so the project architecture is ready for prop projections once a reliable player-level public data source is wired in.

## Why this matters
This is one of the biggest jumps from a simple projection bot toward a more serious betting-research workflow.
